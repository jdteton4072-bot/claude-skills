# Data Sourcing & Cost Analysis Memo
**NFL / CFB Spread & Totals Prediction Service**
Prepared by: Data Sourcing & Cost Analyst | Date: 2026-09-05 | Corrected: 2026-09-05 (citation audit round 1)

---

## Bottom Line

- **Cheapest viable research/backtest stack**: nflfastR/nflverse (free, MIT-licensed code, NFL data 1999+) + CollegeFootballData.com (free/Patreon tiers, CFB PBP 2001+ for FBS) + NOAA/NWS historical observations (free, public domain) + Kaggle "NFL scores and betting data" (free, closing-line-adjacent but **not intraday-timestamped**) for a rough backtest. **This path breaks exactly at timestamped historical odds with opening/movement/closing granularity per book** — the free archives (Kaggle, sportsbookreviewsonline) give only single closing (and sometimes opening) numbers per game, with no intraday timestamps and inconsistent book attribution. Estimated cost: **~$0–$300/yr** (a Patreon tier for CFBD API-tier access), but the resulting backtest **cannot compute true CLV** because you cannot see line movement between open and close.
- **Recommended MVP production tier**: The Odds API (Business, $99/mo) for live + limited historical odds, SportsDataIO or CFBD for stats/injuries, Open-Meteo commercial plan ($29/mo) for weather, self-hosted nflverse/CFBD for historical stats backbone. **Estimated total: ~$3,000–$8,000/yr**, dominated by the odds data line item, which is also the most uncertain because true timestamped historical odds at 5-minute-or-better granularity across multiple books, going back multiple seasons, is either bundled at a premium tier ("Business," $99/mo, per The Odds API, verified 2026-09-05, https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/) or is fully quote-gated (OddsJam, OpticOdds — both "contact sales," unverified $/mo, estimated $500–$1,500+/mo based on third-party benchmarking, **not vendor-confirmed**). **The Odds API's own commercial-use/derived-works terms could not be directly fetched in either research pass (egress-blocked); this recommendation is therefore conditioned on legal review before contracting — see Top Risk #1.**
- **Single biggest licensing risk**: Several odds/stats providers' standard commercial terms either (a) explicitly prohibit use of their data "in a betting, gaming, gambling, or wagering capacity" without separate written approval (Sportradar's non-betting-services terms, verified 2026-09-05), or (b) prohibit commercial/production use altogether on the accessible tier (OpticOdds general Terms of Service bars use "for any revenue-generating endeavor or commercial enterprise" without an upgraded commercial agreement, verified 2026-09-05). A subscription product that publishes picks derived from such feeds could be in direct breach unless a specific commercial/betting-use license is separately negotiated. **[REQUIRES REVIEW BY LICENSED ATTORNEY]** before any launch. A closely related second-tier risk is that **The Odds API's own commercial/derived-works terms remain unverified after two research passes** (see Section 2a and Top Risk #1) — the MVP recommendation depends on this provider and should not be treated as license-cleared.

## 1. Data Requirements Inventory

| Data class | History depth needed | Update frequency | Granularity | Why needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Betting odds — spreads & totals, multi-book, open/move/close, **timestamped** | 10+ seasons where obtainable; NFL further back than CFB is realistic | Live: sub-minute during market hours; Historical: 5–10 min intraday snapshots minimum | Per-book, per-market (spread, total), with opening line, every material move, and the closing line, each with a timestamp | This is the entire basis of CLV, the project's primary success metric. Without timestamped intraday odds you can only ever compare your pick to a single closing number pulled after the fact, not to the market's *path*, and you cannot distinguish a "beat the market early" signal from noise. |
| Game results & box scores (NFL, CFB) | 10+ seasons | Post-game, same day | Team and player level, final and by-quarter | Baseline labels for model training/eval; needed to compute ATS/O-U outcomes. |
| Play-by-play | 10+ seasons (NFL: 1999+ via nflverse; CFB: 2001+ via CFBD, coverage/quality improves post-2013) | Live during games; historical batch post-season | Play-level: down, distance, EPA inputs, personnel, formation where available | Needed to compute EPA, success rate, and other efficiency features that are the analytical backbone of most modern spread/total models. |
| Advanced/efficiency metrics (EPA, DVOA-style, success rate) | Matches PBP depth | Weekly during season | Team/player, per-game and rolling | Buy-vs-build: EPA/success-rate are computable directly from PBP (nflfastR does this natively; CFBD exposes precomputed "SP+"-style and PPA metrics for CFB). DVOA-style opponent-adjusted metrics and PFF grades require either a paid subscription (PFF) or in-house modeling — recommend **compute from PBP** for EPA-class metrics, and treat PFF grades as an optional enrichment, not a dependency. |
| Injuries & depth charts | 3–5 seasons of history sufficient (injuries are a "current state" signal more than a backtest deep-history item); still want as much history as available for research on injury-driven line moves | Live: daily during NFL season (mandated reporting), CFB: irregular | Player-level status (out/doubtful/questionable/probable), body part, practice participation | **Key asymmetry**: NFL has mandated its clubs report practice participation and game status since 1947, with formal daily-designation rules and specificity requirements (verified via NFL Football Operations 2017 injury policy PDF and secondary reporting, 2026-09-05: https://operations.nfl.com/media/2683/2017-nfl-injury-report-policy.pdf). CFB has **no uniform national requirement**; only some conferences (Big Ten, SEC, Big 12) mandate injury/availability disclosure for conference games, and the CFP is reportedly moving to require playoff-game availability reports, but non-conference and many G5 games have no disclosure at all (verified 2026-09-05, CBS Sports / SI reporting). This means CFB injury data will structurally be noisier/sparser and the system must tolerate missing-data gaps that don't exist for NFL. |
| Weather — historical actuals & live forecasts, stadium-level, incl. wind | 10+ seasons for historical actuals at outdoor stadium locations; forecasts only need lookahead of ~5–7 days live | Live: hourly updates in the days before kickoff; Historical: hourly or better for actuals | Stadium-lat/long-specific temperature, wind speed/direction, precipitation, dome/outdoor flag | Wind in particular is a material total-points input for outdoor/cold-weather stadiums; needed both as a live pre-game feature and, historically, as a backtest feature to explain total-line variance. |
| Schedule, venue, surface, travel, rest | 10+ seasons | Static/seasonal, updated on schedule release and any changes | Game-level: kickoff time, venue, surface type (turf/grass), time zone deltas, days of rest since last game | Classic situational features (short week, cross-country travel, surface change) with known effects on spread/total performance; cheap to source and mostly available for free (schedule/venue data is embedded in nflverse and CFBD game-level tables). |
| News & beat-reporter text | Not deep-history critical; want continuous coverage in-season | Live: as-published, ideally within minutes to hours | Article/text plus structured entities (player, team) where possible | Qualitative signal for injuries/depth chart changes not yet reflected in official reports, locker-room sentiment, coaching changes — useful for live retrieval, not for historical backtesting beyond what's needed to validate a text-based feature pipeline. |

**Highest-stakes finding**: every free/low-cost historical odds source located in this research (SBR archives, Kaggle) provides at most an opening line and a closing line per game — **never the intraday path**. True timestamped intraday movement, which is required to validate a CLV-based strategy retrospectively across many books, is only available from paid aggregator APIs (The Odds API's paid historical tiers, OddsJam, OpticOdds, SportsGameOdds), most of which gate pricing behind a sales conversation and several of which carry commercial-use or gambling-use restrictions that would need to be cleared before a paid product could legally use them. This is a real go/no-go risk for the whole business model and warrants direct vendor contract negotiation, not just self-serve signup, before committing to a build.

## 3. Licensing Analysis — Business-Model Risk

**Scraping ESPN / Sports Reference / sportsbook sites — assessment**: The free-data scraping path is **not viable for a commercial product**. ESPN's undocumented endpoints carry no license at all and no stability guarantee; using them in a revenue-generating product exposes the business to both a ToS/legal risk and an operational risk (the endpoint can disappear without notice). Both paths are **viable only for research/prototyping**, not for a production commercial dependency. Any sportsbook site scraping (e.g., to backfill lines) carries the same category of risk plus explicit rate-limiting/IP-blocking enforcement observed on Sports Reference properties, and is reasonably assumed to be similarly or more aggressively enforced on sportsbook operator sites, though this was not independently verified per-operator in this pass.

## 5. Recommendation

### Research/Validation tier
- **Stack**: nflverse (NFL PBP/stats) + CollegeFootballData.com (CFB PBP/stats) + NOAA/NWS (historical weather) + Kaggle/SBR (rough odds context, closing-line-only).
- **Justification**: Zero to near-zero cost, sufficient to prototype features and validate model architecture on results/ATS outcomes.
- **Caveat**: Cannot validate a CLV-based strategy at this tier — treat any "backtested CLV" claim built solely on this stack as unsupported. **Also note: CFBD's terms (Low/Medium confidence, unverified by direct fetch) appear to prohibit reselling/redistributing the raw API data; this tier is internal research use only and should not by itself be read as clearing CFBD for a commercial product — confirm before Tier 2.**

### MVP Production tier
- **Odds**: The Odds API, Business tier ($99/mo, vendor-stated price 2026-09-05) — cheapest self-serve path to a full timestamped historical archive plus live feed, though its per-book depth/reliability for CFB specifically should be spot-checked before committing, **and its commercial-use/derived-works terms remain unverified by direct fetch after two research passes. Recommend treating this as a conditional recommendation: proceed with technical evaluation and trial signup, but do not sign a paid annual commitment or launch a paid public product on this feed until the actual terms document (https://the-odds-api.com/terms-and-conditions.html) has been read and, ideally, written confirmation obtained from the vendor that a subscription picks product is permitted use.**
- **Stats/injuries**: Continue nflverse + CFBD, upgrading CFBD to a paid Patreon tier if quota is exceeded — free/cheap and already covers PBP-derived EPA metrics. **Confirm directly with CFBD that a paid predictions product built on derived outputs (not resold raw data) does not fall under their stated redistribution prohibition before this leaves the research tier.**
- **Weather**: Open-Meteo Standard commercial ($29/mo, vendor-stated 2026-09-05) — cheapest confirmed commercial-use weather license found.
- **News**: Start with RSS + internal text parsing to avoid the $449/mo NewsAPI Business floor (itself unconfirmed by direct vendor-page fetch); upgrade only if structured, licensed news search proves necessary, and re-verify NewsAPI's current price directly before budgeting.
- **Estimated total**: **~$1,600–$7,300/yr** depending on the news-API decision (see Tier 2 cost table). **This figure is a cost estimate only and does not represent license clearance for The Odds API or CFBD — see caveats above.**

### Scale tier
- **Odds**: Negotiate directly with OddsJam, OpticOdds, or SportsGameOdds for a dedicated commercial contract with explicit gambling/derived-works permissions in writing — do not scale on a self-serve tier whose ToS may prohibit the use case.
- **Stats**: Add PFF Pro ($199.99, price vendor-associated but billing period and page itself unconfirmed by direct fetch) or a Sportradar enterprise agreement (only if gambling-use approval is separately and explicitly secured in the contract).
- **Estimated total**: **~$30,000–$100,000+/yr**, highly dependent on negotiated terms.

### Top three cost/licensing risks
1. **Timestamped historical odds is expensive and license-uncertain — and the MVP-recommended provider's own commercial terms are unverified.** The one dataset the entire success metric (CLV) depends on is either bundled at a moderate price (The Odds API Business) or fully quote-gated (OddsJam, OpticOdds, SportsGameOdds) with OpticOdds' own general terms explicitly barring commercial/revenue-generating use absent a separate deal. Critically, **The Odds API's own terms-and-conditions page could not be directly fetched in either research pass** (egress-blocked); the "commercial use permitted, raw resale prohibited" characterization in this memo rests on a secondary search summary only, not a verified reading of the document. **This is escalated here as a go/no-go blocker: do not commit to The Odds API as the MVP odds provider, or launch a paid product on it, until the terms document has been read directly (or the vendor has confirmed in writing) that a subscription picks/predictions product is permitted use. [REQUIRES REVIEW BY LICENSED ATTORNEY]**
2. **Sportradar-class feeds explicitly prohibit gambling-related use and any commercial display/derivative works without written approval** — a subscription product publishing model-derived picks would need a specific, separately negotiated license; using standard non-betting-services terms would be a clear breach.
3. **Free scraping paths (Sports Reference, ESPN undocumented endpoints) are not viable for a commercial product, and CFBD — the recommended free/cheap CFB data source across all three tiers — appears to explicitly prohibit "reselling or redistributing" its API data without permission.** Sports Reference's own ToS explicitly prohibits automated scraping and building a "material substitute" service; ESPN's endpoints are unsupported and legally ungoverned; both are fine for research prototyping only. The CFBD finding is new to this correction pass, is Low/Medium confidence (search-summary sourced, not a direct quote from the terms document), and should be resolved directly with CFBD before the project scales its dependency on this provider past internal research.
